MASTER GUIDE

How to train for 8 days

01

Position

Sit straight. Keep both hands on the home position. The coloured keyboard and hand coach show the exact hand and finger.

02

Learn slowly

Accuracy first. Do not chase speed while learning a new row. Return every finger to its home position.

03

Practice deeply

Give each day at least 4 hours. Split it into focused sessions if needed: learning, repetition, speed and review.

04

Pass the test

Reach at least 15 WPM with strong accuracy. Results are saved on this device so you can see your progress.
